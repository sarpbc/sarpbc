# SARPBC trademark policy

The Apache License 2.0 grants rights to the **source code**. It does **not** grant rights to the SARPBC name, logos, or production domains.

## You may

- Fork, modify, and redistribute the code under Apache-2.0
- State that your project is _based on_ or _forked from_ SARPBC
- Keep copyright and NOTICE attribution as required by the license

## You may not (without written permission)

- Use **SARPBC**, **sarpbc.org**, or confusingly similar names as the primary brand of a competing public site or product
- Use official logos (for example `apps/front/public/sarpbc.png`) in a way that implies you operate the official SARPBC project
- Use production hostnames (`sarpbc.org`, `admin.sarpbc.org`, `api.sarpbc.org`, `t.sarpbc.org`, etc.) for your own deployment
- Suggest affiliation with or endorsement by the SARPBC maintainers when that is not true

## Self-hosting

Self-hosting is encouraged. Use your own domain, branding, and API keys. Do not present a fork as the official SARPBC site.

Questions: open a GitHub Discussion or contact the maintainers via [SECURITY.md](../SECURITY.md) for security-sensitive matters.

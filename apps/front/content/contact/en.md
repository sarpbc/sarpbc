# Contact sarpbc.org

sarpbc.org is an independent Rocket League esports community site. If you need to reach the people behind the project, use the channels below.

## Email

For privacy requests, factual corrections, accessibility feedback, press inquiries, or general questions about the site:

**Email:** contact@sarpbc.org

We read every message. Response time varies with volume; urgent match-data issues on live tournament days may take priority during events.

Please include enough context for us to help: the page URL, what you expected, and what you saw instead. For privacy or account questions, mention the email address tied to your sarpbc.org account when relevant.

## Publisher

The site is published by **Geoffroy Stanger** (see also our [Legal Notice](/legal-notice)).

sarpbc.org is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Psyonix LLC, Epic Games, Inc., or Rocket League.

## Community channels

Some questions are better handled in public so other fans can benefit:

- **Forum:** [Community discussions](/forum) for scene talk, predictions, and site feedback threads.
- **X:** [@SARPBCorg](https://x.com/SARPBCorg) for announcements and quick updates.

For account-specific or private matters, prefer email.

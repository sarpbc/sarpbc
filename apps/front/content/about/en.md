# About sarpbc.org

sarpbc.org is an independent project whose ambition is to build the reference platform for Rocket League esports.

Our goal is simple: create the place where fans can follow the scene, discover its stories, understand what matters, and stay connected to a passionate community.

Inspired by the platforms that changed how fans follow their favorite esports, sarpbc.org aims to give Rocket League the ecosystem a competitive scene of this scale deserves.

---

## Why sarpbc.org?

Rocket League already has an exceptional esports scene.

Talented players, historic teams, international competitions, and a passionate community have kept the game alive for years.

But following that scene is still fragmented. Results, news, stats, and discussion are spread across numerous platforms: Liquipedia, social media, YouTube, Twitch, Discord, and many others.

We believe there should be one central place where the whole community can find what makes Rocket League esports thrive.

That is what sarpbc.org is for.

---

## Our vision

We believe Rocket League still has enormous potential ahead.

A great esport is not only a competitive game or big tournaments. It also grows through the tools that help fans follow the scene, the stories that make people care, and the communities that keep it alive every day.

Our ambition is to contribute to that growth by building a platform that makes Rocket League esports more accessible, more engaging, and more enjoyable to follow.

If more people can discover, understand, and enjoy the competitive scene, we help strengthen the whole ecosystem.

---

## How we build

We want to build sarpbc.org to last.

That means prioritizing useful improvements over big announcements, listening to community feedback, and moving step by step.

Every feature should meet a real need: follow competitions better, understand players and teams more clearly, or simply offer new ways to enjoy Rocket League esports.

sarpbc.org is a project in constant evolution, built with and for its community.

---

## The name sarpbc.org

sarpbc.org refers to _Supersonic Acrobatic Rocket-Powered Battle-Cars_, the game that laid the foundations for Rocket League.

The name represents the origins of a unique community and the story of a game that grew into one of the world's most loved esports.

sarpbc.org is an independent project created by Rocket League esports fans.

We are not affiliated with Psyonix or Epic Games. Our goal is not to represent the official game, but to build tools and an experience for the community around it.

---

## Our principles

### Built for the community

sarpbc.org is built around a simple belief: a strong esports scene needs a platform worthy of its community.

Every decision is guided by one goal: create a better experience for fans by making it easier to access the information, discussions, and stories that make Rocket League esports thrive.

### An independent platform

sarpbc.org is an independent project with no affiliation to Psyonix or Epic Games.

This independence lets us build a platform with a long-term vision, guided by community needs and the evolution of the competitive scene.

### An international ambition

Rocket League is a global esport, with players and fans all over the world.

sarpbc.org is designed to serve this international community. Available today in French and English, the platform aims to gradually expand to other languages to make Rocket League esports accessible to as many people as possible.

### The standards of a real product

We believe a community project should not be limited by its status.

sarpbc.org is built with the same standards as a professional product: a simple, performant, and enjoyable experience, backed by technical foundations that can grow with the project.

### Open and transparent

We believe openness and collaboration can help build better products.

Over time, we want to open parts of sarpbc.org to encourage transparency, enable contributions, and build a project that evolves with its community.

---

## Building the future of Rocket League esports

Building the reference platform for an esport takes time.

We move with a long-term vision: a better experience for following competitions, stronger tools to understand the scene, and an ever more engaged community.

Our goal is that one day, when someone wants to follow Rocket League esports, sarpbc.org is naturally where they start.

Welcome to sarpbc.org.

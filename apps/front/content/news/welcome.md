Welcome to sarpbc.org, your central hub for Rocket League esports.

Our mission is simple: build the HLTV.org of Rocket League. Fast, focused, and made for fans.

Why this site?

Results, news, and community are scattered across Liquipedia, YouTube, and X/Twitter. There's no single place to follow the scene, debate, and share.

What we do

- We collect results, profiles, and stats.
- We publish concise news and explainers.
- We provide a space for fans to argue, celebrate, and trash-talk (all in good fun).

Quick features (live now):

- Team, player and tournament DB (thanks to [Pandascore](https://www.pandascore.co/))
- Daily RL Wordle
- Live forum
- Pick'ems to flex your World Cup IQ

Where this comes from

Since Rocket League joined Epic Games, the competitive calendar lost a split and feature releases have become less frequent. The goal of sarpbc.org is to improve the experience around Rocket League esports, making it easier to follow, discuss, and enjoy the scene. Over time, we hope to expand with even bigger projects and features for the community.

Get involved

We're just getting started. Try the features, join the forum, and tell us what you want next. Your feedback will shape the roadmap!

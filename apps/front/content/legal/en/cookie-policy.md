## What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit our website. They help us provide you with a better browsing experience by remembering your preferences and enabling essential website functionality.

## How We Use Cookies

We use cookies to enhance your experience on our website, analyze website traffic, and understand how visitors interact with our site. This helps us improve our services and provide you with more relevant content.

## Types of Cookies We Use

### Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable basic features like page navigation, access to secure areas, and remembering your cookie preferences. These cookies cannot be disabled as they are essential for the site to work.

### Analytics Cookies

We use analytics cookies to understand how visitors interact with our website. These cookies help us analyze website traffic, measure the effectiveness of our content, and improve our services.

- We use PostHog (EU) for product analytics. Anonymous usage events may be collected without linking them to your account. If you accept analytics cookies, we may identify your session with your account so we can understand signed-in usage. You can change this choice at any time.

## Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have several options for managing cookies on our website:

- Adjust your browser settings to block or delete cookies
- Use our cookie consent popup to accept or decline non-essential cookies
- Use third-party tools like browser extensions to manage tracking

## Cookie Retention

Essential cookies are stored for the duration of your session or until you clear your browser data. Analytics cookies are typically stored for up to 24 months to help us understand long-term usage patterns.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this cookie policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Please check this page periodically for any updates.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies or this cookie policy, please contact us through our website or forum.
